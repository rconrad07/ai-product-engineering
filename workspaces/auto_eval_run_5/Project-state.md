# Project State: IoT Smart Energy Management

## Problem Context

Hotel utility costs are rising, and a significant portion of energy is wasted heating or cooling empty guest rooms. Housekeeping often leaves units running at extreme temperatures after cleaning.

## Business Goal

Implement an IoT-based energy management system that integrates with the Property Management System (PMS). The system should automatically adjust thermostat settings and turn off lights based on real-time room occupancy status and check-in/check-out events.

## Key Constraints

- Must integrate with existing HVAC systems (Zigbee or Wi-Fi enabled thermostats).
- Must detect occupancy without infringing on guest privacy (no cameras).
- Must allow guests to override settings via the in-room thermostat or hotel app.
