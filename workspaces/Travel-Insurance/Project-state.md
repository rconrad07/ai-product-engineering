# Project State

## Objective

Our company currently outsources travel insurance to a third party provider. We want to bring this in house to increase revenue and customer satisfaction.

## Problem Statement

We are giving up too much revenue and customer satisfaction by outsourcing travel insurance and we should bring it in house.

## Assumptions

- We can build a better product than the third party provider
- We can build a more cost effective product than the third party provider
- We can build a more user friendly product than the third party provider

## Constraints

- Time: We need to launch this product within 6 months to capture the holiday travel season.
- Scope: Initial release will target US Associates using the company website.
- Technical: We will use the existing booking engine and payment gateway.
- Business: We will need to work with the legal team to ensure compliance with all regulations and actuarial requirements.

## User Personas

-As a frequent traveler, I want to be able to purchase travel insurance at the point of booking so that I can have peace of mind during my trip.
