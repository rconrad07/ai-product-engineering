# Market Research: Total Upfront Pricing

## 🔍 Competitive Landscape

Major players in the travel industry have already begun adapting to "Honest Pricing" regulations:

- **Airbnb:** Introduced a "total price" toggle globally, showing the full price inclusive of all fees (except tax) in search results.
- **Marriott & Hilton:** Have updated their US-based search results to include resort fees in the initial rate for most jurisdictions, following pressure from several State Attorneys General.
- **Expedia/Booking.com:** Largely displaying total price in the EU (due to stricter regulations) and rolling out similar features in the US.

## 📈 Consumer Sentiment

- Guests are increasingly frustrated with "Drip Pricing."
- Studies show that while higher initial prices may slightly reduce click-through rates (CTR), they significantly increase the "Select-to-Book" conversion rate, as there is no sticker shock at the end.
- Transparency is becoming a key brand differentiator.

## ⚖️ Regulatory Trends

- **California SB 478 (Effective July 1, 2024):** Prohibits advertising a price that is less than the actual price a consumer must pay (excluding government taxes).
- **FTC "Junk Fee" Ruling:** The FTC is actively working on a federal rule to ban hidden fees across all industries.
- **Global Outlook:** EU and UK already have strict all-in pricing requirements. We should aim for a global standard to simplify backend logic.

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations

1. **Marketing 'Honest Pricing':** Turn the change into a positive marketing message. "The price you see is the price you pay."
2. **Comparison Tools:** Ensure that we handle cases where competitors might still be hiding fees, perhaps by adding a "Competitive Transparency" badge or tooltip.
3. **Consistency:** Ensure the same "Total" is shown across all channels (API, Web, Mobile Web) to maintain price integrity.
