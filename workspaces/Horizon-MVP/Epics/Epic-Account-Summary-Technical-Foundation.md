# Epic Template

**Description:** Horizon / ARN MVP — implement the technical foundation for the **Account Summary** experience, including viewing upcoming and past reservations and enabling key self-service actions such as cancelling a reservation and updating basic account information (for example, name, email, and address), built on the Mako-based platform.

---

| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Problem Being Solved:** | ARN guests currently have limited or inconsistent ability to view and manage their reservations and basic account information in a way that aligns with the new Mako-based platform. To make Horizon MVP viable and reduce support overhead, we need a reusable Account Summary experience where guests and internal users can reliably see reservation details and perform core self-service actions without relying on manual processes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Success Metrics**       | * For MVP test cohorts, a high percentage of supported reservation changes (for example, cancellations within allowed rules) can be completed via the Account Summary page in non-production environments. * Internal support contacts report reduced need to manually intervene in scenarios covered by Account Summary actions. * Account Summary surfaces reservation and profile information consistently across devices, using shared platform and design system patterns.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Specific Use Case(s):** | Using the Gherkin format, document the scenario(s) as a series of steps that describe the actions and expected outcomes. * `Scenario`: ARN guest views Account Summary and cancels an eligible reservation * `Given`: The guest is authenticated and has at least one upcoming reservation that is eligible for cancellation under business rules * `And`: The BFF exposes endpoints to retrieve account profile information and reservations for that guest * `When`: The guest navigates to the Account Summary page * `Then`: The frontend requests and displays basic account information (for example, name and email) and the list of upcoming and past reservations * `And`: For an eligible upcoming reservation, the guest selects a “Cancel Reservation” action and confirms * `Then`: The BFF validates eligibility and calls downstream services (for example, TCMP or equivalent) to process the cancellation * `And`: On success, the reservation state is updated and the Account Summary view reflects the change; on failure, a clear error message is shown and the event is logged. * `Scenario`: ARN guest updates basic account information from Account Summary * `Given`: The guest is authenticated and the BFF exposes endpoints for updating basic profile attributes (for example, name and address) * `When`: The guest edits their profile fields on the Account Summary page and submits changes * `Then`: The BFF validates and persists the updates via appropriate downstream services * `And`: The Account Summary view refreshes to show the updated information or indicates any validation errors. |
| **Release Plan:**         | Initial Release vs Full Release vs Site Hierarchy Release: * Initial Release: Account Summary available in non-production environments for a limited set of test users and reservation scenarios, focused on viewing reservations and performing basic cancellation and profile updates. * Full Release: Out of scope for this Epic; expanded self-service capabilities and broader rollout can be covered by future Epics. * Site Hierarchy Release: Initial enablement for ARN MVP siteIDs and domains that participate in Horizon MVP flows. Proposed date when the feature flag should be flipped and for whom: When upstream Account Summary dependencies (authentication, reservations, and profile services) are stable enough to exercise end-to-end flows in POC. Plan to remove the feature flag, if applicable: To be determined as part of a broader Horizon rollout plan; for now, Account Summary access remains gated to test cohorts and internal users.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Sites Impacted:**       | ARN Horizon MVP siteID(s) and domains where authenticated users will access Account Summary in non-production environments. Future production rollout and additional brands or partners are out of scope for this Epic but should be supported by the underlying design.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
