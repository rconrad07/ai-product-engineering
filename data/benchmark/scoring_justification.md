# Hospitality Tech SUPR-Q Scoring Justification

This document provides the rationale for the synthetic scores assigned to each competitor.

## 1. Google Hotels (Google)

- **Overall Score:** 4.75/5
- **Notes:** Industry benchmark for speed and integration. The map-centric UI and price tracking are best-in-class. Trust is massive due to the Google brand and verified reviews.

| Subscale | Score | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Usability** | 5.0 | Seamless integration into search; zero-friction desktop experience. |
| **Trust** | 4.5 | High trust in price accuracy, though ads are clearly labeled. |
| **Appearance** | 4.5 | Clean, Material Design aesthetic. Professional and modern. |
| **Loyalty** | 5.0 | High habitual use; the primary starting point for travel research. |

---

## 2. Airbnb (Airbnb)

- **Overall Score:** 4.56/5
- **Notes:** The gold standard for hospitality UX. High visual appeal and professional design. Navigation can occasionally be tricky with map-only filters.

| Subscale | Score | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Usability** | 4.5 | Great search flow, though category-based search is sometimes restrictive. |
| **Trust** | 4.0 | Strong brand but occasional variability in listing quality vs. luxury rivals. |
| **Appearance** | 5.0 | Beautiful imagery and minimalist typography. |
| **Loyalty** | 4.75 | Extremely high NPS; strong community moat. |

---

| Subscale | Score | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Usability** | 4.0 | Similar logic to Expedia; stable and easy to navigate. |

## 7. Costco Travel (Costco)

- **Overall Score:** 3.63/5
- **Notes:** High friction due to login requirements and legacy UI. However, trust and value are off the charts for members. It doesn't look pretty, but you trust it completely.

| Subscale | Score | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Usability** | 2.5 | Forcing login and multi-step validation is a major UX hurdle. |
| **Trust** | 5.0 | Costco's "No-questions-asked" brand trust carries over. |
| **Appearance** | 3.0 | Legacy aesthetics; high information density, low styling. |
| **Loyalty** | 4.0 | Members are extremely loyal to the "Costco Value" proposition. |

---

## 8. Sam's Club Travel (Walmart)

| Subscale | Score | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |

| **Loyalty** | 3.0 | Transactional; less emotional loyalty than Costco members. |
