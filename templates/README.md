# templates

Purpose
- Canonical templates used for planning and work-handling: PRDs, Jira tickets, design briefs, and acceptance criteria formats.

What belongs here
- PRD templates (product requirement documents)
- Jira ticket templates (issue descriptions, AC checklist)
- Design brief templates
- Acceptance criteria formats and example entries

Suggested layout
- /templates/prd/
- /templates/jira/
- /templates/briefs/
- /templates/acceptance-criteria/

Guidance
- Keep templates opinionated and minimal — make it easy to copy-paste and adapt.
- Include example-filled templates to demonstrate expected level of detail.
